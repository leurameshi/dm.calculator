How to Calculate BMI (Simple Step-by-Step)

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a quick way to know if you're at a healthy weight.

BMI Formula

The calculation is done by dividing weight by height squared.

Practical Example

A person weighing 70kg and 1.70m tall will have a BMI of 24.22.

BMI Table

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5-24.9: Normal weight
  • 25-29.9: Overweight
  • 30+: Obese
