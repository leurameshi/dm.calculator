How many liters of water should I drink per day?

The ideal amount of water varies from person to person, but a common recommendation is to consume about 35 ml of water per kilogram of body weight.

For example, a person weighing 70 kg should drink approximately 2.45 liters of water per day. This value may vary depending on routine, ambient temperature, and physical activity level.

Why is drinking water important?

Water is essential for the human body's functioning. It participates in various processes, such as digestion, blood circulation, and body temperature control.

  • Helps hydrate the skin
  • Improves bowel function
  • Aids in toxin elimination
  • Contributes to proper organ function

What happens if I drink too little water?

Lack of water can cause symptoms such as fatigue, headache, dizziness, and difficulty concentrating. In more severe cases, it can lead to dehydration.

Tips for drinking more water

  • Carry a water bottle with you
  • Set reminders throughout the day
  • Consume water-rich fruits
  • Avoid sugary drinks

Staying hydrated is essential for daily health and well-being.
