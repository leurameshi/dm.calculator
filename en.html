What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure used to assess whether a person is within the ideal weight for their height. It is widely used by health professionals as a simple way to identify possible weight-related risks.

Although it is not a definitive diagnosis, BMI is an excellent initial indicator to understand whether you are underweight, of normal weight, overweight, or obese.

---

How to calculate BMI?

The BMI calculation is done by dividing the weight (in kilograms) by the height (in meters) squared.

For example: a person weighing 70kg and 1.70m tall will have a BMI of approximately 24.22.

How to improve your BMI?

Maintaining a healthy BMI is essential to avoid health problems. Some important tips include:

  • To have a balanced and nutrient-rich diet
  • Engage in physical exercise regularly
  • Avoid ultraprocessed foods
  • Drink plenty of water throughout the day
  • Maintain a quality sleep routine

Small changes in daily life can already generate big results over time.

Why monitor your BMI?

Regularly monitoring your BMI helps identify changes in your body and allows you to take action quickly to keep your health on track.
