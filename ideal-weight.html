What is the ideal weight for my height?

The ideal weight varies according to height, age, sex, and body composition. One of the most used methods to estimate ideal weight is through BMI (Body Mass Index).

How to calculate ideal weight?

A common way to estimate ideal weight is using the average healthy BMI value, which is between 18.5 and 24.9.

For example, a person with a height of 1.70m can have a healthy weight between approximately 53 kg and 72 kg.

Factors that influence ideal weight

  • Age
  • Sex
  • Physical activity level
  • Muscle mass
  • Genetics

Why maintain ideal weight?

Maintaining weight within a healthy range helps reduce the risk of various diseases, such as diabetes, hypertension, and cardiovascular problems.

Tips for maintaining a healthy weight

  • Exercise regularly
  • Eat a balanced diet
  • Avoid excessive processed foods
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Have a good sleep routine

The most important thing is to maintain healthy and consistent habits over time.
